Local to Lagos

Painting & decorating in Lagos

Lagos is a popular base for expats on the western Algarve coast, with a historic old town and surrounding villa developments. Whether it's a villa, apartment, holiday rental or commercial premises, the work is quoted around the condition of the actual surfaces — and every step is explained in English.

Lagos combines a historic old town — narrow streets, a marina, apartment buildings — with newer villa developments spreading out toward Meia Praia and the surrounding hills. That mix means different priorities depending on where a property sits: old-town facades are often older render on traditional construction, where crack repair and breathable coatings matter more than colour choice, while standalone villas further out are usually a straightforward exterior repaint plus a long run of boundary wall, shutters and railings that have been taking the coastal sun for years.

Painting in Lagos

What to know before painting in Lagos

Property Types & Profile

Properties range from apartments and townhouses in the historic old town to standalone villas spreading out toward Meia Praia, Porto de Mós and the hills above the marina. Old-town buildings typically have traditional render over older masonry, painted frontages directly onto the street, and timber shutters and balcony ironwork facing the sun. The newer villa developments sit on individual plots with private driveways, gardens, pool terraces and considerably more wall area — including boundary walls that are usually in worse condition than the house itself. Marina-front apartments form a third category, generally with balcony metalwork taking the full brunt of salt air off the water.

What Owners Tend to Ask About

Owners who split time between Lagos and the UK or Ireland tend to worry most about arriving to find the property has visibly aged in the months they were away — chalky walls, greyed shutters, rust bleeding from a balcony rail. In the old town, working on a facade that fronts directly onto a narrow street brings access and neighbour considerations that a villa plot simply does not have. Around the newer developments near Meia Praia, the bigger question is usually the sheer amount of wall — house, boundary, pool terrace — and working out what genuinely needs doing this year rather than all of it at once.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about Lagos

Do old-town properties in Lagos need a different approach to the villas near Meia Praia?

Generally yes. Old-town buildings tend to be older construction where breathable coatings and proper crack repair matter more, and where access is off a narrow public street. Villas near Meia Praia are usually straightforward access on a private plot, with far more total wall area including boundary walls and a pool terrace.

How much does salt air off the marina actually affect a property?

Most on metalwork and least on rendered walls. Balcony railings, window grilles and ironwork on marina-front and seafront properties corrode noticeably faster than the same metal a few streets inland, which is why they usually need attention on a shorter cycle than the walls do.

Can you paint a facade that fronts directly onto a narrow old-town street?

Yes — it is common work in Lagos. It needs planning around access and pedestrians rather than simply setting up as you would on a private plot, so the approach gets agreed before anything starts.

Is a hillside villa above Lagos more exposed than a property in town?

Usually yes. An elevated, open plot takes more direct sun and more wind-driven rain on its exposed elevations, which is why one side of a hillside villa often looks noticeably older than the others.

Should the boundary walls be done at the same time as the house?

It is usually the sensible call, particularly on the Meia Praia and Porto de Mós plots where the boundary wall is what the property is seen as from the road. Boundary walls are also generally in worse condition than the house, because nobody looks at them closely until they are being quoted.

Do the timber shutters on an old-town property need different treatment?

They need the same approach as anywhere in the Algarve — sanded back to sound timber and given a finish that can move with the wood — but old-town shutters are often older and have been recoated more times, so there is more to take off before you reach sound wood.

Can work be scheduled around a holiday rental in Lagos?

Yes. Rental properties here are commonly done in an off-season window or a changeover gap, with the scope set to what genuinely fits the time rather than overrunning into the next booking.

Is it worth painting a marina-front apartment terrace separately from the building?

Often yes — the building facade is usually a condominium decision, while your own terrace walls, balcony rails and shutters are yours to deal with. Those are also the parts taking the direct salt exposure.

What condition are Lagos properties usually in when they are first assessed?

It varies more by exposure than by age. A sheltered elevation can look fine while the south or west wall on the same property is chalking badly — which is why quoting elevation by elevation tends to be more honest than quoting the property as one number.
